Minimal Pro

Minimal Pro adds strict mode and more than one focus schedule. Every new install includes 7 days of Pro, with nothing to buy. After that, Pro is available as:

  • Monthly or Yearly — auto-renewing subscriptions. The yearly plan may include a free trial for new subscribers.
  • Lifetime — a one-time purchase.

Prices are shown in the app before you buy and may vary by country.

Subscriptions

  • Payment is charged to your Apple ID when you confirm the purchase.
  • A subscription renews automatically unless it's cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the period ends.
  • You can manage or cancel subscriptions in your App Store account settings after purchase.
  • Any unused part of a free trial ends when you buy a subscription.

Using Minimal

Minimal helps you pause apps and notice how you use your phone. It relies on Apple's Screen Time features, which iOS controls, so it can't guarantee that an app is always blocked. It isn't a parental control, and it never affects emergency calls.
