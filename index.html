Your phone, quieter.

Minimal turns your home screen into a calm list of words, and puts a mindful pause in front of the apps that pull you in.

What it does

  • A text launcher for your home screen — the time, one line of focus, your apps as words.
  • A pause before distracting apps: it asks why now, gives you a breath, and lets you choose how long.
  • When your minutes are up, it asks how it went — and lets you stop there.
  • Insights that make the minutes mean something: today against a usual day, your hardest hours, the time your pauses won back.

What it doesn't do

No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. Everything stays on your iPhone.
